Frequently asked questions

Common questions about alerts, consent, and how the service works.

What is Handshake Alerts?
Handshake Alerts is a notification service that texts you when Handshake AI projects you add have more than two claimable tasks waiting.
Is this affiliated with Handshake?
No. Handshake Alerts is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Handshake.
How often do you check for tasks?
On the interval you choose per project (for example about every 10, 20, or 30 minutes).
When will I get a text?
When we find more than two claimable tasks on a project where the alert is on and you still have alert credits.
How do I stop texts?
Reply STOP to any message, turn the alert off for a project in your dashboard, delete the project, or sign out.
What kinds of texts will I get?
Account notification texts when Handshake AI projects you enable have claimable tasks. These are not marketing messages.
